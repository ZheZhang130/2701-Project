# Distinct driver demo sequences

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Each column of the contact
sheet is one fictional driver, and each row is a different staged moment.
The app consumes individual extracted cells, not the full contact sheet.
These are synthetic images and manual class annotations, not model predictions.

Prompt:

Create ONE photorealistic contact sheet for a driving-monitoring demo, exactly
3 columns and 6 rows, 18 equal rectangular panels, edge-to-edge regular grid no
gaps no borders no text. Each COLUMN is a different fictional adult driver and
each ROW is a later moment. Column 1: woman age 30 dark hair ponytail wearing
blue denim shirt in gray sedan. Column 2: man age 40 short dark hair beard wearing
burgundy polo in black sedan. Column 3: woman age 45 curly brown hair wearing
green sweater in beige sedan. Strictly preserve each column's person's identity,
face, clothing, car, fixed passenger-side dashboard camera angle and daylight
across all six rows. Camera sees face, torso, steering wheel and hands in every
panel. These are staged parked-car educational reenactments. Row 1 all drivers
looking forward hands on wheel. Row 2 all looking down operating smartphone in
right hand. Row 3 all phone held at right ear, left hand on steering wheel.
Row 4 all right hand raising a cup toward mouth and eyes off road. Row 5 all
turned toward back seat reaching behind with right arm. Row 6 all texting with
phone low in right hand, face visibly looking down. Every panel must be a
genuinely different pose and action, not zooms or duplicate images. Exactly
3 columns by 6 rows. High quality realistic photography, natural anatomy,
no lettering or watermarks.
